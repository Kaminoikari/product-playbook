# Recipe: Full Product Plan

**When to suggest this:** the user wants an end-to-end plan for a new product or a large new capability, from strategy through handoff.

## Lens sequence

1. strategy-kernel
2. persona-journey
3. opportunity-solution-tree (optional)
4. jtbd
5. problem-framing
6. positioning (optional)
7. pr-faq
8. solution-prioritization
9. mvp-scoping
10. success-metrics
11. pmf-gtm (optional)
12. prd-and-handoff (optional)

Steps marked `(optional)` may be skipped when the deliverable does not need them. Jumping back to an earlier lens after new evidence surfaces is expected; re-run only the lenses whose inputs changed.

This is a suggested order, never forced. It is not a slash command; the meta-skill offers it in one line and proceeds on the user's own sequence if they prefer.
